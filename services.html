Services

Secure integration and governance for AI-enabled operations

Global Tunnels combines legacy system modernization, data management, secure cloud operations, and AI governance for organizations with strict compliance needs.

Core services

From legacy systems to governed AI

01

AI Infrastructure and Integration

We architect the data pipelines, API gateways, role-based access controls, and cloud foundations needed to connect AI models to enterprise systems without uncontrolled exposure of sensitive data.

  • Legacy Oracle, SQL, document, and application integration
  • API gateway and middleware architecture
  • Identity-first security and Zero Trust Architecture environment separation
  • Secure cloud deployment and DoD-aligned DevSecOps pipelines
02

AI Governance, Risk, and Compliance

We help organizations design the policy, access-control, and audit-logging layer that governs how AI is used, and evaluate AI systems against recognized risk and compliance frameworks.

  • Policy design and access controls for AI usage
  • Audit logging and evidence for compliance review
  • NIST AI RMF-aligned assessments and risk documentation
  • AI red-team and prompt-injection testing
  • Organizational AI policy and operating procedures
03

Data Management and Modernization

AI depends on reliable data. We support collection, validation, transformation, governance, security, access, and quality assurance across multi-source environments.

  • Data governance and quality processes
  • Dashboards, analytics, and operational reporting
  • Secure data storage and controlled sharing
  • Modernization of business applications and workflows
